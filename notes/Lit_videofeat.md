## Video features in EthoGraph — takeaways

**Two-tier extractor design, three named extractors.** Video features become a name-keyed slot in `VideoFeaturesConfig` (`extractor: s3d | timm | feral`), all writing the same `(time, dims)` sidecar that `merge_video_features` samples onto the trial clock. Two kinds, following `v-iashin/video_features`' taxonomy (copy the design, not the package — it isn't pip-installable, uses top-level `models`/`utils` names, and dumps frames to disk): clip-wise (S3D; `stack_s` applies) and frame-wise (`timm`; `stack_s` rejected). The frame-wise route is `timm.create_model(model_name, pretrained=True)` + `reset_classifier(0)` + the model's own `resolve_data_config` transforms — ~20 lines, one optional dependency (`ethograph[timm]`), and it covers DINOv2, DINOv3, ConvNeXt, EVA, etc. without per-model code. Cheap-tier default when `model_name` is omitted: `vit_small_patch14_reg4_dinov2.lvd142m` (Apache 2.0, free download, 22M params, 384-d CLS per frame, laptop/CPU-runnable at reduced `analysis_fps`). DINOv3 is the same string-swap (`vit_small_patch16_dinov3.lvd1689m`, or `convnext_small.dinov3_lvd1689m`), identical quality to Meta's release, but its weights are gated behind licence acceptance + HF login — so not the default. Embed the individual's crop (reuse the SPOT ROI), not the full frame. Precedent: DINOv2+LSTM ethograms (Cell Rep Methods 2025), Edinburgh Pig DINOv2/v3 + BiLSTM. S3D stays because it works on my data; compare all three with `compare_runs` on the same trials.

**Frozen FERAL as the expensive tier.** FERAL (pip-installable, MIT, "lite" variant) fine-tunes a V-JEPA2 backbone with attention pooling + linear head. Its backbone needs no retraining to give features: run it frozen, export the pooled pre-projection per-frame embedding (averaged across overlapping chunks), optionally also the C-dim class probabilities as a small second sidecar. These feed ASFormer/MS-TCN alongside kinematics like any other feature block — reduce dimensionality (PCA to ~32–64) so 1024-d doesn't swamp ~30 pose columns. Frozen V-JEPA2 should beat S3D and DINOv2 on motion-driven behaviour. If FERAL is *fine-tuned*, embeddings must be produced per CV fold to avoid leakage; frozen is the safe default. Backbone choice is delegated to FERAL's ablation — no backbone zoo to defend.

**FERAL as a model, not just a feature extractor.** Beyond the sidecar path, integrate FERAL as an architecture that directly predicts labels end-to-end from video, alongside the pose-based models — "one default per slot": kinematics → ASFormer, video → FERAL. Cheapest first test of whether video helps: late fusion, averaging FERAL's frame probabilities with the pose model's, no retraining. Frozen backbone + head hooks also give the before/after-fine-tuning embedding visualisation for the GUI without a separate ViT branch. Audio features (VGGish in video_features) stay out of this slot — if wanted later, an `AudioExtractor` on its own clock.
